The Target
The target equals 2/3 of the average of all group choices.
🏆
The Winner
The player closest to the target wins the game!

Suppose 4 players choose: 20, 30, 40, and 70.
• Group average: (20 + 30 + 40 + 70) / 4 = 40
• Target (2/3 of the average): 40 × 2/3 = 26.67
👉 The player who chose 30 is closest to 26.67 and wins!
